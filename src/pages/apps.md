They all need **macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer**.

## Where the names come from

All three are named after songs by Stephen Cogle and Peter Stapleton. crocus and jonah are [Victor Dimisich Band](https://thebigcity.co.nz/artists/v/victor-dimisich-band/) songs, and mekong is from "Mekong Delta Blues", which they also recorded as [Scorched Earth Policy](https://thebigcity.co.nz/artists/s/scorched-earth-policy/). The most recent versions of all three are on [An Afternoon With Victor Dimisich](/blog/an-afternoon-with-victor-dimisich.html), the collection of 1981 demos that Siltbreeze released this year.
