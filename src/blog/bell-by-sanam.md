Here's the start of what I hope will become a series of more regular posts highlighting tunes and albums I like. This one comes care of some sunny evening DJing by one Aaron Hawkins (esquire) at The Duck in Macandrew Bay.

This song "Bell بل" is by Beirut band "<a href="https://sanambeirut.bandcamp.com/music" title="More Sanam music on Bandcamp" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sanam</a>" off their 2023 album "<a href="https://sanambeirut.bandcamp.com/album/aykathani-malakon" title="The album at Bandcamp" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Aykathani Malakon</a> صنم - أيقظني ملاكٌ".  They are known for "blending Arabic poetry and traditional folk songs with psychedelic rock, krautrock, free jazz, and electronics" and this song contains all that.
