Colin and Malka have released another superb <a href="https://swimhq.bandcamp.com/album/what-is-lost-will-return" title="Bandcamp page for the Immersion album" target="_blank" rel="noopener">album</a>, following on from 2025's great <a href="https://immersiohq.bandcamp.com/album/wtf" title="Bandcamp link to WTF album by Immersion" target="_blank" rel="noopener">WTF??</a>. This has the recognisable full bass sound of Malka (which I love), Colin's textured guitar, various synths, noises, and Holy Fuck's excellent drummer Matt Schulz providing the percussion where needed.

They both sing or talk on the same track, which I find endearing and a solid division of labour. Like the previous album, it seems they are both trying to reconcile the shitscape that is now our world in 2026.

Highly recommend.
