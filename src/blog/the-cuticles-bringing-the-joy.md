<a href="https://cuticles1.bandcamp.com/" title="Official Bandcamp profile for The Cuticles" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Cuticles</a> channel a chaotic Christchurch '80s sound (think a blend of Scorched Earth Policy and The Builders) from their base between Dunedin and Christchurch. They are poppy, noisy, intense, and they don't mess around with most songs clocking in at the 2 minute mark. The band amazingly toured France recently, and played a killer <a href="https://www.undertheradar.co.nz/gig/102587/The-Biscuits-Cuticles-And-Stef-Animal.utr" title="UTR page on the gig" target="_blank" rel="noopener">show</a> in Dunedin with The Biscuits and Stef Animal two weeks ago.

They have a new album called <a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/album/cuticles-more-joy-lp" title="Link to Bandcamp for the album" target="_blank" rel="noopener">More Joy</a>, their second on esteemed Chicago label <a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/" title="Official Bandcamp for Siltbreeze Records" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Siltbreeze</a>, following on from 2023's <a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/album/cuticles-major-works-lp" title="Link to Bandcamp for Major Works album" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Major Works</a>. More Joy has a massive 21 tracks while the earlier record had 18 but none of it drags –&nbsp;they don't mess around! There have been some personnel changes in between albums and judging by the quality of More Joy, the band is the better for it.

<a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/track/ogled-moat" title="Link to Ogled Moat on Bandcamp" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ogled Moat</a> is the third track off the album and sums up both the band and album well. If you like it, buy their record and go see them.
