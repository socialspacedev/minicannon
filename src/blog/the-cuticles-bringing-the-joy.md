The Cuticles channel a chaotic Christchurch 80s sound from their base between Dunedin and Christchurch. They are fast, poppy, noisy, and a bit shouty but they don't mess around with most songs around the 2 minute mark.

They have a new album called "<a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/album/cuticles-more-joy-lp" title="Link to Bandcamp for the album" target="_blank" rel="noopener">More Joy</a>", their second on esteemed Chicago label <a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/" title="Official Bandcamp for Siltbreeze Records" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Siltbreeze</a>. Matthew Plunkett is the main fella.

<a href="https://siltbreeze.bandcamp.com/track/ogled-moat" title="Link to Ogled Moat on Bandcamp" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ogled Moat</a> is a great track off the new album that gives you a clear flavour of what to expect.
