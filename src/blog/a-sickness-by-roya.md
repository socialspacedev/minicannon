Here's an amazing live performance of a kickarse three chord tune called "A Sickness" by defunct NY band "<a href="https://www.wonderlandmagazine.com/2017/03/23/premiere-roya-sickness/" title="Short piece on the band" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Roya</a>". While the band was driven by vocalist Rahill Jamalifard, drumming was provided by much missed <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2022/12/09/arts/music/hamish-kilgour-dead.html" title="NY Times obituary about Hamish" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Hamish Kilgour</a> ... well until he was kicked out that is.

A Sickness was included on their solitary self-titled <a href="https://www.discogs.com/master/1198061-Roya-Roya?srsltid=AYCX9vL368dR4wp4Qqt0vPsBHeZ_v-KIZHbeYzaCrj9zNE42QSg7cISf" title="Link to Discogs page on the album" target="_blank" rel="noopener">album</a> from 2017. I particularly love this live version because you get the full Hamish percussion experience. Keep an eye on his shoulders (when he appears) and the tempo speeding up as the song progresses. Fans of The Clean will particularly dig it.
